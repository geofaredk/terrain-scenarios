description=Change the terrain as easily as you'd sketch on a map. Draw a line or an outline, choose what should happen (a dike, a ditch, a pond, a levelled pad) and the DEM updates immediately. You don't need a raster calculator, masks or any GIS expertise, and the original DEM is never touched. Use the modified DEM wherever terrain matters: flood and drainage modelling, landscape and nature-based solution design, earthworks planning, or simply showing what a place could look like. Export the modified DEM as a GeoTIFF for flood and drainage modelling, landscape design, earthworks or visualisation. Requires the Python package scipy, which is included in the standard QGIS installers for Windows and macOS; on Linux, install it with your package manager (e.g. python3-scipy).
